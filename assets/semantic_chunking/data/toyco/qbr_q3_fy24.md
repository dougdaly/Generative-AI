# ToyCo – Q3 FY24 Quarterly Business Review
--- Slide 1 ---

## Overview

Q3 FY24 was a steady quarter with no major surprises. Total revenue grew modestly, driven by Enterprise subscriptions and a gradual shift from Retail to Online.

(Chart: Total revenue by segment – not included in export)

--- Slide 2 ---

## Revenue by Segment

Segment      Q3 FY23    Q3 FY24    Δ YoY
-----------------------------------------
Enterprise   118.2      126.4      +6.9%
SMB           44.7       45.2      +1.1%

Enterprise revenue grew 6.9% year-over-year, driven by expansion deals in North America. SMB revenue was essentially flat, with low single-digit growth and no significant pricing actions.

--- Slide 3 ---

## Channel Performance

Channel      Q3 FY23    Q3 FY24    Δ YoY
-----------------------------------------
Online        79.5       86.8      +9.2%
Retail        83.4       84.8      +1.6%

We continue to see a gradual shift toward Online, especially in Enterprise self-serve. Retail remains stable but is not a growth driver.

--- Slide 4 ---

## Churn & Retention

Metric                 Q3 FY23     Q3 FY24
------------------------------------------
Enterprise churn        6.1%        5.8%
SMB churn              13.4%       13.2%

Churn patterns remained within historical ranges. Small-business retention improved slightly due to a focus on onboarding and basic success playbooks.

There were **no meaningful pricing changes** in Q3 FY24, and we did not observe any unusual spikes in cancellations.

--- Slide 5 ---

## Key Takeaways

- Enterprise growth remains the main driver of revenue.
- Channel mix continues to shift slowly toward Online.
- SMB churn is elevated compared to Enterprise but stable and predictable.
