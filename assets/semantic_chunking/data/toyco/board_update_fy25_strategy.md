# ToyCo – FY25 Strategy Update (Board Draft)

## Long-Term Trends (Last 2 Years)

Over the past eight quarters we have seen:

- Enterprise revenue growing 6–8% YoY consistently.
- A gradual shift in channel mix toward Online, now >50% of total revenue.
- SMB churn remaining elevated vs Enterprise but mostly stable in the 13–15% annual range.

From a distance, the business looks stable and predictable.

## Recent Anomalies

However, FY25 to date has two notable deviations from this pattern:

1. **SMB churn spike in Q1 FY25**  
   Following the April price increase on SMB Online self-serve plans, churn in that cohort briefly exceeded 20% annualized. This spike was concentrated in:
   - Month-to-month small-business accounts,
   - Promo-heavy acquisition cohorts.

2. **Short-term Online revenue uplift**  
   The same price action generated a short-term bump in Online revenue growth, which makes top-line performance look stronger than the underlying customer dynamics.

These events are not visible if you only look at high-level revenue charts. They require reading the churn analysis and post-mortem documents in detail.

## Board-Level View

From a board perspective:

- Enterprise remains a solid, boring engine.
- SMB is where the risk and upside are:
  - Pricing, packaging, and messaging changes can move churn and growth meaningfully.
  - Our current experiment improved ARPU but may have hurt long-term LTV for certain small-biz cohorts.

## What We Are Watching

- Whether SMB churn normalizes by Q3 FY25.
- LTV/CAC for SMB cohorts acquired under different pricing regimes.
- Mix between Online and Retail, especially for small-business customers.

If SMB churn remains elevated, we will revisit the FY25 pricing strategy and consider more gradual changes.
