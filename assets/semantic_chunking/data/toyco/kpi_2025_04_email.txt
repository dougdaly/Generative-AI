Hey team,

Quick April readout before the full Q2 FY25 deck:

- Top line looks good on the surface: Online revenue is up again vs March.
- However, small-biz retention is noisy. We're still digesting the April price increase.

A few messy notes (data team is still cleaning this stuff up):

* Online SMB cancellations jumped right after we rolled out the new pricing page.
* It's mostly month-to-month self-serve accounts churning, especially those that originally came in through promo-heavy campaigns.
* Enterprise and larger SMB accounts look fine; no unusual churn there.
* Retail is boring as usual – flat to slightly down.

This feels like a smaller repeat of what we saw in Q1 FY25 right after the first round of pricing changes. We'll know more once we get a full quarter of post-change behavior.

More in the retrospective doc, but tl;dr:
- Short-term revenue lift: yes.
- Long-term LTV/CAC impact: unclear.
