# CMS Coverage — Arthrocentesis
Source: https://example.gov/cms/arthrocentesis
Coverage: Arthrocentesis of a major joint (CPT 20610) is covered when medically necessary.

Requires a diagnosis consistent with joint effusion, adhesive capsulitis, or rotator cuff pathology
(e.g., ICD-10 M75.0x for frozen shoulder or M75.1x for rotator cuff tear).

Imaging is not required if physical exam clearly documents effusion and functional limitation.

Use modifier 50 if the procedure is performed bilaterally and both joints meet medical necessity criteria.
