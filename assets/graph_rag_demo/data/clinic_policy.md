# Clinic Policy (Internal)
- Saturday surcharges apply for procedures performed in hospital outpatient or ASC settings; no surcharge for in-office procedures.
- Nerve conduction studies (e.g., CPT 95900) generally require prior authorization unless diabetic neuropathy (ICD-10 E11.4x) is documented and payer policy explicitly waives prior auth.
- For bilateral procedures, append modifier 50 and document laterality.
- We are conservative when ambiguous.
